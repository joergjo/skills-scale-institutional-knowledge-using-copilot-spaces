# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation hub. This directory serves as the central repository for all key processes, templates, and best practices that guide our project delivery at OctoAcme.

## Purpose of This Copilot Space

This Copilot Space and the `docs/` folder are designed to centralize and standardize program and process documentation across OctoAcme. By maintaining all project management processes in a single, accessible location, we enable:

- **Knowledge centralization**: A single source of truth for how we run projects
- **Faster onboarding**: New team members can quickly understand our approach
- **Consistent practices**: Teams follow the same processes and use the same templates
- **AI-powered assistance**: Copilot Spaces can use these docs as context to provide role-specific guidance
- **Continuous improvement**: Processes can be easily updated and shared across teams

## Overview of Project Management Processes

OctoAcme uses an iterative, outcome-oriented approach rooted in customer value, clear ownership, data-driven decisions, and rapid feedback. Our methodology emphasizes psychological safety, encouraging teams to learn from failures and continuously improve their delivery practices.

At the core of our approach are five key principles: customer-first (prioritizing customer value and usability), iterative delivery (delivering small, testable increments), clear ownership (each project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead), data-informed decisions (measuring impact and iterating based on evidence), and psychological safety (encouraging feedback and learning). These principles shape every phase of our project lifecycle.

Our project lifecycle consists of five distinct phases. During **Initiation**, teams validate the business need, align stakeholders, and define success criteria through a lightweight project one-pager. The **Planning** phase involves detailed scope definition, resource allocation, milestone setting, and risk/dependency management. Throughout **Execution & Tracking**, teams build and test features iteratively using agile boards, maintaining clear acceptance criteria and quality standards while monitoring velocity and escalation paths. The **Release & Deployment** phase ensures thorough pre-release checks, documented deployment procedures, and rollback plans. Finally, **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement** captures learnings, tracks action items, and feeds improvements back into future projects.

Communication and collaboration are critical to our success. We maintain a regular cadence of weekly syncs between Project Managers and Product Managers, twice-weekly standups for delivery teams, and monthly stakeholder updates. Each project is supported by clearly defined roles—Project Managers coordinate delivery and manage risks, Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize the backlog, Developers implement features and ensure quality, QA validates acceptance criteria, and Stakeholders provide input and approvals.
